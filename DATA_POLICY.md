# Data policy

## Privacy
This work is based on real student submissions. Raw submissions (DOCX/PDF/TXT/images) are **private** and must not be committed to this repository.

## Allowed public artifacts
We recommend publishing only **derived anonymized artifacts**, such as:
- per-assignment score tables (no raw text)
- anonymized identifiers (hashes instead of MSSV)
- audit logs/summary statistics
- aggregated metrics (QWK/MAE/Pearson) and plots

## Not included
- dataset_clean/, dataset_20260321/
- any original submission files
